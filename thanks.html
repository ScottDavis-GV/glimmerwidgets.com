Getting your license key…

This page shows your Glimmer Pro license key after you buy, and confirms a renewal. The link that brought you here has no order in it.

If you have just paid, write to hello@glimmerwidgets.com with your Stripe receipt and we'll send your key.

We need to look at your order before it's complete. There's nothing you need to do: we'll be in touch by email.

Questions in the meantime go to hello@glimmerwidgets.com; include your Stripe receipt.

Adding the key to Glimmer

  1. Open the License keys window: right-click Glimmer's tray icon and choose Settings ▸ License keys…, or Glimmer Pro ▸ Add license key… if the menu shows it.
  2. Paste the key into License key and choose Add.
  3. If Glimmer offers Restart now, choose it to turn on your Pro widgets.

Adding a key needs an internet connection, once per computer. A key works on up to three of your computers. To move it to another, remove it in License keys on the old one first.

Save this key now, for example in a password manager. It is not emailed to you, and this page shows it only for a day. If you lose it, write to hello@glimmerwidgets.com with your license ID or your Stripe receipt and we'll send it to the email address you bought with.

Getting your renewed key

For your security, a renewed key is not shown here. Glimmer collects it, using the key it already has:

  1. Open the License keys window (Settings ▸ License keys… in the tray menu) on a computer where your Glimmer Pro key is added.
  2. Choose Check for updated key.

Do this on each computer you use; it doesn't use up another computer. If your key isn't added anywhere just now, write to hello@glimmerwidgets.com with your license ID and Stripe receipt.
