Glimmer Pro

Build status

Every repository you work on, one line each, worst result first, from GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, TeamCity and Jenkins, in the cloud or on your company's own server. You see a broken main branch before your teammates have to tell you.

The result that matters

Each repo shows the worst of its workflows' latest runs on the default branch. Failing sorts to the top, then running, then passing.

Told when it changes

A notification when a build starts failing, and a quieter one when it recovers. Several changes at once arrive as one.

One click to the run

Click a row or a notification to open that run on GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, TeamCity or Jenkins.

Light on your API limits

Checks every 60 seconds with conditional requests, so an unchanged repo doesn't use your GitHub rate limit.

Calendar-aware

Next meeting

A countdown to your next meeting, with a Join button, so the meeting doesn't start without you.

One click to join

Google Meet, Zoom, Teams and Webex links, found in the event's conference details, location or description.

A five-minute warning

A reminder before each meeting, and the tray turns amber until it starts.

The one after, too

Shows what's on now, what's next and what follows, or a single line when collapsed.

Same calendar, no extra setup

Reads the calendar the timebox already reads, in your system's clock format.

Glimmer Pro

Unpushed work

The work that lives only on this computer. Uncommitted changes, commits not pushed, branches that never left your machine.

Every repo, at a glance

Scans your project folders every five minutes and lists only the repos with something at risk. Clean ones are counted, not listed.

More than the current branch

Other branches with unpushed commits, branches never pushed, stashes, and branches whose remote was deleted.

Leave the archives out

Right-click a repo to exclude it, or the whole folder it lives in.

Never in your way

Read-only git commands that don't lock your repo, so it never blocks the commands you run yourself.

Glimmer Pro

PR queue

What's waiting on you: pull requests that ask for your review, and your own that are ready to merge or need another push.

Reviews, oldest first

Requests made to you or one of your teams, the longest-waiting at the top.

Yours, by what they need

Checks failing, changes requested, conflicts, ready to merge or still waiting: one clear state per pull request.

Told when it's your turn

A notification for a new review request, or when one of yours gets changes requested or starts failing.

One read-only sign-in

Sign in once through the Glimmer GitHub App, shared with build status, with its own choice of account. Already use the GitHub CLI? Its accounts work too. GitLab merge requests and Bitbucket pull requests are in the same list.

Glimmer Pro

Stand up

How long you've been sitting, and a gentle reminder to stand up that knows about your meetings and focus sessions.

Never mid-meeting

It waits for a meeting to end, or comes early when one starts in ten minutes: "Stand up before your 2:00 meeting."

Respects your focus

During a timebox session it waits for the shutdown phase, but never past 90 minutes of sitting.

Notices your breaks

Lunch, a locked screen or five minutes away from the keyboard counts as a break, so it never nags you just after you came back.

Gentle, not pushy

A silent notification, Done, 5 more minutes or Skip, and a short stretch suggestion. No streaks, no guilt.

Glimmer Pro

Day's end

At a stop time you choose, what's still open from your other widgets, so you stop cleanly instead of whenever you look up.

One checklist for the day

Repos not pushed, builds failing, pull requests waiting on you, and today's handoff notes, from the widgets you run.

A heads-up first

Fifteen minutes before your stop time the panel glimmers and says what's open, so there's time to wrap up.

All clear, or not

The tray turns amber only at the end of the day, and only while something is still open.

Done for today

One click closes the day; no more reminders until your next working day.

Tomorrow

Write down what to do first tomorrow while it's still obvious, and find it waiting for you in the morning.

Written at the end of the day

A short list you write and edit yourself, with today's handoff next steps offered to add.

First thing in the morning

On your next working day the panel becomes Today, first, glimmers, and shows the first item.

Nothing lost

Plan tomorrow carries over whatever you didn't tick. The list never moves on by itself.

Part of Day's end

Glimmer Pro

Journal

What you did today, written down as it happens and corrected by you. Ready for a standup, a timesheet, or the Friday when Tuesday is a blur.

Writes itself

Focus sessions, handoff notes, meetings, builds fixed, work pushed and review requests cleared go into the day's log as they happen.

Yours to edit

Change or remove any line, add your own, and keep notes for the day. Step back through earlier days.

Plain Markdown files

One file per day in a folder you choose, such as your Obsidian vault. Readable anywhere, for years, with or without Glimmer.

Reports, written for you

A weekly report ready to email to your supervisor, and a review-year report with your highlights for performance-review time.

Glimmer Pro

Service status

Is it you or them? The status pages of the services you depend on, in one panel, so you know GitHub is down before you start debugging your push.

The services developers use

GitHub, npm, Cloudflare, OpenAI, Claude, Vercel, Netlify, Supabase, Sentry and more, ticked in a menu. Add any other Statuspage page by its address.

Only the parts you use

Watch a whole page, or just the components you depend on, so a data centre on another continent doesn't turn your tray amber.

Told when it breaks

A notification when a service you watch has an outage, with the incident's title. One click opens it.

No sign-in

Reads each page's public status every five minutes. Nothing about you is sent.

Glimmer Pro

Expiry watch

Your domains' certificates and registrations, with a warning while there's still time to fix them, not the morning the site goes down.

Certificates and domains

For each domain you add: when the certificate it serves expires, and when the registration runs out, straight from the registry.

Warned at the right time

Certificates renew themselves, so it warns 14 days out, when renewal has clearly failed. Domains get 30 days, time to sort out a card or a registrar.

Once, not every day

One notification per stage, and a renewal clears it. The tray stays red until it's fixed.

No sign-in

Reads the certificate your site already shows everyone, and the registry's public RDAP record.

Better together

Separate widgets that know about each other when you own more than one.

At the timebox's shutdown phase

Unpushed work rescans and comes forward if anything isn't pushed: "Before you stop: 2 repos with work not pushed."

During a focus session

Good-news updates, like a build recovering, wait until you stop. A newly failing build still gets through.

At the end of the day

Day's end gathers what's still open from Unpushed work, Build status and the PR queue, today's handoffs become suggestions for Tomorrow, and the journal writes the day down.
