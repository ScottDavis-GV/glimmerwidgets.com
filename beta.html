Thank you for testing Glimmer

For beta testers · updated 29 October 2026

The short version: install Glimmer, add the beta key from your invitation, use it during your normal working days for two or three weeks, and tell us what helps, what annoys you and what breaks, with Send feedback… in the tray menu. Nothing is sent to us unless you send it.

Getting started

  1. Download the newest beta (marked Pre-release) from the releases page: Glimmer-Setup-…-x64.exe for most Windows computers, …-arm64.exe for Windows on Arm, or the .AppImage or .deb for Linux.
  2. Run it. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, check that the publisher reads Scott Davis, then Run anyway.
  3. Right-click the tray icon and choose Glimmer Pro ▸ Add license key…. Paste the beta key from your invitation and choose Add. It works on one computer.
  4. In Choose widgets, tick what you'd like to try. You can change it any time from Widgets ▸ Choose widgets…
  5. Make sure Settings ▸ Get beta versions is ticked, so each new beta reaches you by itself.

The guide explains every widget, and each panel's ☰ menu has How it works…

What to try

Use Glimmer as you'd normally work; these are suggestions, a week at a time.

Week 1: focus and the dock

  • Run a few timebox sessions a day, and write the handoff note at the end. Is the shutdown warning early enough? Too pushy?
  • Arrange the panels: drag them out, use the house button, drag one between two others, try another corner in Settings ▸ Home.
  • Turn on Stand up and see whether its reminders come at sensible times.

Week 2: your work

  • Connect Build status and the PR queue to GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket, and Build status to TeamCity, in the cloud or your company's server (how). Do the right repositories show, and do the notifications come at the right moments?
  • Point Unpushed work at your project folders. Does it find everything, and nothing it shouldn't?
  • Set a stop time in Day's end, and write tomorrow's first steps in Tomorrow.

Week 3: the record of your work

  • Let the Journal run all week, then open ☰ ▸ Reports ▸ This week's report. Would you send it? What's missing?
  • Try Service status with the services you rely on, and Expiry watch with a domain you look after.
  • Adjust ☰ ▸ Notifications for each widget until Glimmer is quiet enough.

Known issues

  • Calendars: connecting Google Calendar or Outlook doesn't work in this beta yet. Use the timebox's ☰ ▸ Timebox calendar ▸ Sample week to see how calendar-aware sessions and Next meeting behave; real calendars come in a later beta.
  • SmartScreen and antivirus: Glimmer is new, so Windows and some antivirus may be cautious about it until it builds a reputation. The publisher must read Scott Davis.
  • Linux: on GNOME the tray icon needs the AppIndicator extension.
  • macOS isn't available yet.

Sending feedback

Choose Send feedback… in the tray menu or in any panel's ☰ menu. It opens an email to hello@glimmerwidgets.com in your own mail program, with your Glimmer version, Windows version and the widgets you run filled in. Glimmer doesn't send anything itself, and there's no telemetry.

  • Say what happened and what you expected. A screenshot helps (Win+Shift+S).
  • Small annoyances are as useful as bugs: a reminder at the wrong moment, a label that confused you, a panel in the way.
  • Ideas are welcome too, including widgets you'd want.
